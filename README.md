# Seating Charts

Exam seating for campus rooms, for TAMU instructors: upload class rosters, seat sections in rooms (spaced out or side by side), print seating charts, room rosters, version lists and exam labels, and email each student their seat from your own TAMU Gmail.

**Download:** the newest version is always at the top of [Releases](https://github.com/cpsinkule/seating-charts-releases/releases/latest). This page holds only the finished apps.

## Installing Seating Charts

Seating Charts runs on your own computer and opens in your web browser. Installing takes a couple of minutes, and you
never need a terminal. Your rosters and charts stay on your computer.

Download one file from the [latest release](https://github.com/cpsinkule/seating-charts-releases/releases/latest)
(under **Assets**):

- **Mac:** `SeatingCharts-<version>-mac.dmg` (Apple silicon Macs: M1 or newer)
- **Windows:** `SeatingCharts-<version>-windows-setup.exe`

### Mac

1. Double-click the **.dmg** file. In the window that opens, drag **Seating Charts** onto the **Applications** folder.
2. Open your **Applications** folder and double-click **Seating Charts**.
3. The first time, your Mac says *"Seating Charts" Not Opened* because the app isn't registered with Apple.
   Click **Done** (not Move to Trash).
4. Open **System Settings** (Apple menu › System Settings), choose **Privacy & Security**, and scroll down to
   **Security**. Next to *"Seating Charts" was blocked to protect your Mac*, click **Open Anyway**, then confirm with
   your password or Touch ID, and click **Open Anyway** once more.

From then on, Seating Charts opens with a double-click like any other app. You can keep it in the Dock: right-click
its Dock icon › Options › Keep in Dock.

### Windows

1. Double-click **SeatingCharts-…-windows-setup.exe**. If your browser warns that the file isn't commonly downloaded,
   choose **Keep** (in Edge: ⋯ › Keep › Keep anyway).
2. If Windows says *Windows protected your PC*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click through the installer (**Next**, **Install**, **Finish**). It doesn't need an administrator password.

Seating Charts is then in the Start menu, and on your desktop if you left that box ticked.

### Using it

- **Start:** double-click **Seating Charts**. A small window says *Seating Charts is running*, and the app opens in your
  web browser. If you close the browser tab, click **Open Seating Charts** in that window to get back.
- **Quit:** click **Quit** in the small window (or close it). Your work is saved as you go.
- **Help:** the **Help** page in the app explains each step, from rooms to printing and emailing students.
- **Email students:** the first time, the app walks you through connecting your TAMU Gmail and sending yourself a
  test email.

### Updating

When a new version is out, Seating Charts shows a banner at the top with a **Download** button. Quit Seating Charts,
then install the new version the same way: on a Mac, drag it into Applications and choose **Replace** (your Mac may
ask for **Open Anyway** once more); on Windows, run the new installer. Your exams, rooms and settings are kept.

### Uninstalling

Mac: drag Seating Charts from Applications to the Trash. Windows: Settings › Apps › Installed apps › Seating Charts ›
Uninstall. Your data stays in its folder (shown in the app's small window) until you delete it.
